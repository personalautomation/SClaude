# Privacy

> **What goes to SClaude's owners? Nothing.**

- **No telemetry.** SClaude sends nothing to its authors: no usage statistics,
  no crash reports, no analytics.
- **Your services, your logins.** What it sends goes only where your work
  already goes: Anthropic (as with any Claude Code use), your product's GitHub,
  your deploy target, and your own Telegram bot if you set one up.
- **Your data stays on your machine.** Session transcripts, terminal
  recordings, each feature's history and its metrics stay local, unless you
  name a team repository to gather the metrics in. Working files are kept out
  of git and out of every deployment.
- **Nothing is exchanged with the public repository unless you choose it.**
  Taking updates and sending fixes back are each a setting you pick at install.
  A fix is checked for your product's details first, and by default nothing
  leaves without your yes.
- **Nothing listens on the internet.** The per-feature addresses answer only
  your machine and your private network; the phone's gate opens only while you
  have phone access turned on.

The full account: [Privacy: what leaves your machine](https://github.com/personalautomation/SClaude/blob/main/docs/privacy.md).
