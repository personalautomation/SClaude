4. How a session changes sclaude

Any session marked W may take this way: it changes sclaude in its tree's workshop, and sclaude publishes the change once the session ends.

5. The friction review

Every session marked F leads here once it ends: what the safety rules refused it is read by a session of its own, which lifts a rule, leaves it, puts it off a month, or fixes sclaude.
